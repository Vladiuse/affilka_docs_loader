This section allows you to create links and invite affiliates into your affiliate program. This section is particularly useful for affiliate managers who represent your affiliate program on various iGaming and affiliate forums and who want to invite new partners to come and promote your brands.

To create a link, press “New”. Copy the link you have created by clicking. Copy button in the third column and give it to a potential affiliate you want to refer to your program or paste it on some gambling forum to allow multiple affiliates to register with your affiliate program.

- **Visits count**column shows the amount of unique visits of the invitation link.
- **Referred partners**column shows the amount of affiliates registered via the invitation link. By clicking the number of referred partners you will be transferred to the List of Partners (/casino/partners) page filtered by the Invite link ID..

All affiliates who registered via this link will be attached to your dedicated operator profile – so in a way you will be their dedicated affiliate manager.

**IMPORTANT! ****Referral links serve as an additional marketing tool for operators to invite new partners, it has nothing to do with sub-affiliation.**
