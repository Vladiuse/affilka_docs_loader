The “News” page is located under the “Affiliate Program” navigation tab and can be reached via the **/casino/news** link.

Here operators can create their affiliate program's news and share them with their affiliates. You can use this section to notify your partners about such events as:

- a new brand was added to your affiliate program
- your terms and conditions were updated
- your team is participating in an iGaming expo
- you have launched a jackpot, a tournament, or have added a long-awaited game to your iGaming brand – all of which can be used to boost player acquisition and conversion
- you want to advertise CPA and Hybrid deals as individual commission plans available to your affiliates along the default revshare plan
- technical maintenance
- etc

On the **/casino/news **page you will find a complete list of all news articles created within your affiliate program. At the top of the table you will find 4 tabs filtering the news by their state: All, Published, Draft and Archived. 

- **All**: contains all news articles created within your affiliate program regardless of their state.
- **Published:**contains only those news articles that were published and are now visible to your partners in the Affiliate Interface.
- **Draft:**contains news articles in a “Draft” state. These articles haven't been published yet and are not visible to your affiliates.
- **Archived:**contains news articles that were archived. They are not visible to your affiliates.

You can filter and find the news articles by their ID, Name, Author, Date of creation and publication.

In order to create news you need to click on the "Create" button in the top right corner of the “News” page (**/casino/news**).

Fill in the form on the News creation page (all the fields are marked as required):

- **Name:**the news name that will be displayed only in the Operator Interface.
- **Preview banner:**the preview banner which will be reflected on the Affiliate's Dashboard, News list and News detailed page.- *Recommended banner's aspect ratio is 16:9.*
- **Header:**the news title that will be displayed in the Affiliate Interface.
- **Content:**a WYSIWYG text editor where you create the news content. Thanks to the WYSIWYG editor you can see and edit the news content in the same form as it would later be displayed in the Affiliate Interface, You can find a text editor control panel just under the "Content" field. With its help you can set text headings, alignments, colours, font styles, create lists, add links and upload images, etc.

Click on the "Save as a draft" button in order to create a news draft.

As soon as you've created your news draft, you can do one of the following actions with your draft:

- **Publish:**when you click this button, the news article will be published in your Affiliate Program and will be visible to all of your affiliates.
- **Archive:**when you click this button, the news article will be archived. If the news article was published, it will be deleted from the affiliate interface and will not be visible to affiliates. This action is irreversible.
- **Edit:**if you want to change or modify your news draft, you need to use this action. You can edit your news article even if the news is published. The changes will be applied immediately.

Once the news is published, the affiliates can see it on the Affiliate Dashboard under the "Latest news" section and on the "News" list page (/partner/news) under the "Affiliate program" tab.
