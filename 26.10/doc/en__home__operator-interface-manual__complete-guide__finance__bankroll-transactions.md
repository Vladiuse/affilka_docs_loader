Those operators who rely on Affilka merchants when it comes to processing affiliate payments, can view all transactions on their affiliate program bankroll. Bankroll transactions can be found in the "**Finance**" tab on the **/casino/bankroll_transactions **page.

Bankroll decreases when affiliate payments are approved (payment amount and payment commission are subtracted from bankroll). Bankroll increases when a previously successful payment was refunded back to an affiliate account.

Bankroll can also be topped up and decreased manually by the Affilka financial managers – you will see it on balance transactions page under manual transaction type. You can filter and find the required transactions by using filters on the left-hand side and export all bankroll transactions in CSV format.

You can filter bankroll transactions by:

- **Action**(dropdown selector): choose whether money was added to or subtracted from the affiliate program bankroll.
- **Reference**- **type**(dropdown selector): choose which transaction type changed the bankroll. It can be a payment, a payment commission, or a manual bankroll change by a financial manager.
- **Currency and amount (from…to):**indicate the transaction amount in money equivalent. If you need to find all bankroll transactions of over, say, 20 EUR, choose EUR in the “Currency” filter and type 20 in the “Amount:from” filter.
- **Created at date:**indicate the date range when a transaction occurred.

You can view a detailed bankroll transaction page by clicking on the Bankroll Transaction ID in the bankroll transactions table.

This page contains the following information about the bankroll transaction:

- **Bankroll**- **Transaction**- **ID**– a unique identifier of the transaction.
- **Action**– the type of action (either “Add” or “Subtract”). Shows you whether money was added to or subtracted from the affiliate program bankroll.
- **Amount**– the amount of the transaction.
- **Bankroll amount before and Bankroll amount after**– the amount of funds available on the affiliate program bankroll before and after the transaction.
- **Reference**– the transaction type. It can be a payment, a payment commission, or a manual bankroll change by a financial manager.
- **Actor**– the person or entity who made the transaction.
- **Created**- **at**- **date**– the date when the transaction was made.
- **Comments**– any comments associated with this transaction made visible by financial managers to the affiliate program operators.
