Balance corrections page can be reached via the **/casino/balance_corrections** link.

Here operators can view and filter all balance corrections that were previously created by correcting partner's balance and see who and when performed the correction, whether money was added to or subtracted from partner account, what kind of correction it was (e.g. advance payment, manual cashout, etc). The table can be exported into CSV format.

- **Partner ID**(full match mode, does not support multiple values): type the unique partner identifier here and find a partner on whose account the balance correction was made;
- **Action**(dropdown selector): choose whether money was added to or subtracted from partner account;
- **Kind**(dropdown selector): choose what the reason behind the correction was; whether it was some marketing expense, manual cashout, promo, advance payment, or something else;
- **Currency and Amount (from…to)**: indicate the amount of balance correction in money equivalent. If you need to find all corrections over, say, 20 EUR, type EUR in the “Currency” filter and type 20 in the “Amount:from” filter;
- **Brand**(dropdown selector): choose the brand where the balance correction was made;
- **Correction created at (from...to)**: indicate the date range when a balance correction was performed;
- **Operator ID**(full match mode): type the unique operator identifier here and find which operator was responsible for the correction;
- **Reporting period (from....to):**indicate the dates to which a balance correction can be attributed as a marketing expense. You can read more on reporting period here.

You can view a detailed balance correction page by clicking on the balance correction id in the balance corrections table.

This page contains detailed information about the balance correction. Here you will see the Balance correction ID, date and time when this correction was made, the reporting period to which this balance correction applies, the info on balance correction amount in fiat or crypto currency; brand on which the balance correction was performed, the partner whose balance was changed, the operator who changed it, what the action was (Add or Subtract types), the kind of the correction (marketing expense, manual cashout, promo, advance payment, or something else) and a comment added by the operator to this balance correction.

**The Reporting Period **indicates the dates to which a specific marketing expense can be attributed. Reporting period consists of 2 dates: start and end date.

**The Reporting period** for balance corrections is set manually when a balance correction is created. The period can be set for past, present and future periods. The end date cannot be earlier than the start date but they can coincide. 

*Use case examples:*

- *listing fee: in October 2020 you've agreed that you will make an advance payment of 10000 EUR to the affiliate for placing your casino ad in "Best New Casinos" section on their website for 3 months starting from November 1, 2020. When making a positive balance correction, indicate the start date as November 1, 2020 and the end date as January 31, 2021. The reporting period thus will be in future.*
- *bill correction: the commission plan which you have assigned to an affiliate was configured incorrectly. Therefore the bill amount is incorrect as well. You cancel the bill and make a positive balance correction. Indicate the reporting period in the past - the dates should coincide with the reporting period of the aforementioned bill.*
- *terms violation: an affiliate has violated the terms of the affiliate agreement. They've already accrued some funds on their balance. You subtract the funds and indicate the reporting period either in present or in the past, depending on what period the violation occurred in.*

Reporting periods are added to balance corrections table in the Operator Interface and can be extracted with other data in CSV format. You can also filter balance corrections by their corresponding reporting dates using filters on the left.
