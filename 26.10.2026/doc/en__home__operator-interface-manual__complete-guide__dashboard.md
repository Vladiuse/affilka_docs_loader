This is the starting page of the Operator Interface. Here you will get an outline of your affiliate program’s bankroll and partner balances, quick stats and charts, pending cashouts and commission bills as well as latest events. The Dashboard will be improved further to reflect a more concise and accurate picture of what’s going on within your affiliate program at a quick glance.

**PLEASE NOTE!** You will be able to access the product functionality only after you enable 2FA authentication. Right after the initial login, you need to scan the generated QR-code in the authenticator app, then enter it in the “Code” field and click “Confirm”.

Bankroll is the liquidity of funds available within your affiliate program. Bankroll currencies coincide with your affiliate program base currencies. As a rule, an affiliate program has 2 base currencies: one for fiat currencies and the other for crypto. However, there may be more base currencies than just two. All affiliate cash-outs are made from your bankroll. If there is not enough liquidity, you will not be able to process payments to affiliates.

Bankroll amount decreases when you approve cash-outs (payment amount and payment commission are subtracted from bankroll). Bankroll amount can also decrease when a manual bankroll deduction is performed by Affilka financial managers (e.g. you request a specific payment to be made from your affiliate program bankroll by sending us an invoice).

Bankroll is topped up manually by Affilka financial managers. You send us the funds and we add them to our merchants and reflect the amount on your bankroll.

This section is available to those Operators who use our merchants to process affiliate payments. Operators with their own merchant accounts do not see it.


Here you will see the amount of money all of your partners have on their balances. Affiliates can request a withdrawal of these funds at any moment. Affiliates can have accounts in as many currencies as you have as your base currencies.


Here you can see a quick picture of players’ activities across all of your affiliates, brands, and products within the affiliate program. The statistics are converted into your base fiat currency. The data can be displayed for the current day, week, month, or year, and for the ended periods: yesterday, last week, month, or year. The calendar periods are calculated here. To get a more detailed report, click on the “Full statistics” link in the top right corner of the section or visit **/casino/report.**

Every operator can customize the Quick Stats on the Dashboard up to their liking by clicking on the "Gear" button in the top right corner of the section. The system will remember their preferences upon future logins (until further change). You can indicate up to 9 fields for the Quick Stats section.

You can indicate one or several brands for filtering in the quick stats section. By default quick stats show the info on all of your brands.

In addition to that, you can indicate a custom period (from...to dates) for analysis.

A graphic representation of all players’ activities across all of your affiliates, brands and products within the affiliate program. The statistics is converted into your base fiat currency.

You can choose which data you want to see in the chart by choosing the required metric from the drop-down selector on the left.

You can indicate one or several brands for filtering in the quick charts section. By default quick charts show the info on all of your brands.

There is also a second line within the chart that shows the analogous ended period (e.g. last week, month, year). To disable the second line, switch off the “Period Comparison” toggle switch in the bottom right corner of the section. In addition to that, you can indicate a custom period (from...to dates) for analysis. Once you do that in the Quick Charts section, you will find yourself on a tab with your indicated custom period and the "Period Comparison" option will be automatically turned off – so you will only see one line on the graph.

To get a more detailed report, press the “Full Statistics” link in the top right corner of the section.
