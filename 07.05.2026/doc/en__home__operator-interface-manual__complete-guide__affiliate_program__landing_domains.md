A landing domain is the base/root domain of your Igaming brand on which landing pages are created and hosted. Landing pages are specific URLs under that domain – that's where affiliates redirect players.The landing domain identifies the site, while the landing page represents a specific path on the website.

**Example:**

In the landing page `https://casinosite.com/christmas_bonus`, the landing domain is `https://casinosite.com`.

This page contains a list of all landing domains within your affiliate program. The list of landing domains is divided into 2 tabs: active and archived.

- **Active**: contains all landing domains based on which you can create landing pages for your affiliates.
- **Archived**: contains all archived landing domains that can no longer be used for creating landing pages. An archived landing domain always means that all its landing pages are archived as well and are not visible to affiliates when creating campaigns and promos.

You can filter the landing domains by:

- **IDs**. unique landing domain identifiers. Supports multiple values.
- **Names**: custom names assigned to landing domains. The filter supports multiple values and an autosuggest feature. You can select several names from the drop-down selector and filter the landing domains by them.
- **Brands**. brands to which landing domains are connected and to which the end-users will be referred. Please, note, that sub affiliate landing domains are not connected to brands but to the whole affiliate program. The filter supports multiple values and an autosuggest feature. You can select several brands from the drop-down selector and filter the landing domains by them.
- **Subaffiliate (All/Yes/No):**If a landing domains is designed to attract subaffiliates, choose “Yes”. If the landing domain isn't designed for subaffiliates but for players, choose “No”.
- **URLs**. The URLs of the landing domains. The filter supports multiple values and an autosuggest feature. You can select several URLs from the drop-down selector and filter the landing domains by them.

To create a landing domain, press “New” in the top-right corner of the page. Fill in the form:

- **Name**– A custom name for internal reference.
- **Subaffiliate**– Enable this option if the domain is intended for referring sub-affiliates.
- **Brand**– Select the brand this domain is associated with (not applicable for sub-affiliate domains).
- **Landing domain**– Enter the full domain URL (must use- `https://`protocol and without paths, e.g. “- `/`” or “- `/bonus`”, etc.).

Click "Submit" button.

Right after you create a landing domain, the landing page with the same URL will be created and will be available on the "Landings" page (/casino/landings). During landing creation (/casino/landings/new), recently added landing domain will be available under “Domain” field. 

If this is the first landing page for the brand, it will be automatically marked as **Default**.

to archive a landing domain, press “Archive” next to the landing domain. The date when the landing domain is archived will be logged and displayed in the “Archived” landing domains tab under the “Archived at” column.

When you archive a landing domain, **all landing pages** created under this domain will be automatically archived. These landing pages will no longer be visible to new affiliates and will no longer be available for new campaigns or promos. Nonetheless, they will remains active for the old campaigns where they are already used.
