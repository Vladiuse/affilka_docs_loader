On the **/casino/partners** page you will find a complete list of all partners registered within your affiliate program. At the top of the table you will find 4 tabs: all, verified, not verified and blocked. 

- **All**: contains all partner accounts registered within your affiliate program.
- **Verified**: contains only those partner accounts which you and your team have verified. These affiliates can create campaigns and referral links to promote your brands. It is also possible to set up automatic verification. In this case all affiliates will be verified automatically upon registration.
- **Not verified**: these are affiliate accounts waiting for verification. Without verification, affiliates cannot promote your brands or access their partner profiles.
- **Blocked**: these are affiliate accounts that you have blocked. These partners cannot access their partner profiles at all.

This area represents a paginated list of partners, showing 20 results on each page.

The table can be viewed in full-screen mode if you click on the full-screen button in the top right corner of the table.

You can export the list of partners in CSV format.

You can filter partners using the filters on the left. The following filters are available:

- **Partner IDs:**unique partner identifier. Supports multiple values.
- **Partner emails:**partner email submitted at registration or added as part of partner’s team members emails. Supports partial search and multiple values.
- **Company names:**the name of the company indicated by an affiliate at registration. Supports partial search and multiple values.
- **Microsoft Teams:**affiliate account name in Microsoft Teams (former “Skype”). It's a text input field which supports partial search.
- **Telegram:**affiliate telegram account name or phone number. It's a text input field which supports partial search.
- **Traffic**- **sources:**affiliate traffic sources indicated by an affiliate. It's a text input field which supports partial search.
- **Dedicated operator emails:**emails of dedicated affiliate managers assigned to specific partners. The filter supports multiple values and an autosuggest feature. You can select several dedicated operators from the drop-down selector and filter the partners by them.
- **Tags:**custom tags assigned to partners by affiliate managers for easier navigation. This filter supports multiple values and an autosuggest feature. Start typing the name of the tag and it will appear in the dropdown selector. You can select several tags and the filter will find all partners who have all of the tags you've indicated.
- **Commission ID:**Filters partners by currently assigned specific active commissions. As you type, an autosuggest list will appear; you can select multiple commissions to include in your search.
- **Invited by link**– this filter allows you to include partners who were only invited via operator invitation link created on the- __Invite Affiliates__page or exclude them from the List of partners.
- **Invite affiliate link IDs**– ID of a link operator used to invite a partner.
- **Balance currency and balance:**these filters allow you to find partners who have funds on their balance. If you need to find partners who have at least 20 EUR on their accounts, choose “EUR” from the dropdown selector in the Balance currency filter and indicate “20” in the “Balance from” filter. Leave “Balance to” empty.
- **Players count:**allows you to filter partners by the number of players introduced to your iGaming websites.
- **Depositing players count:**filter partners by the number of depositors introduced to your iGaming websites.
- **Partner created at:**allows you to filter partners by their registration date.
- **Archived:**allows you to filter partners by their archived status: All, Yes or No. By default the system shows only non-archived partners.
- **Report settings:**allows you to filter partners by the type of report visibility settings applied to them: Default or Custom.

To apply filters, click on the “Filter” button at the bottom of the Filters section. To clear the filters, press “Clear”.

Transactions tab will take you to all balance transactions (don’t confuse them with balance corrections!) which happened on the accounts associated with this partner. All balance transactions are recorded as double book entries where every transaction has at least one pair. For example, when a pending bill is approved, it is removed from the Bills account and credited to the Partner Account. So the transactions should be analysed from the Account Type perspective rather than affiliate program versus affiliate entities.

__The following Account Types exist:__

- **Partner**- **Account**: it's an account associated with partner balance. To this account the money is added when you approve a Bill. Partner Account changes when you subtract or add funds manually via Balance Correction. Partner Account changes when an affiliate requests a cash-out of the available funds from their balance.
- **Bills Clip Account**: it's an account associated with this partner's bills. Bills Clip Account increases when a Bill is issued. It decreases when the Bill is approved or cancelled.
- **Partner Debt Account:**it's an account associated with partner debts resulting from negative carryover. It increases when the negative carryover accumulates. When the negative carryover is offset by a positive reward, the amount on the account decreases.

__The following Reference Types exist:__

- **Balance Correction**: it can be either a positive or a negative balance correction performed with affiliate balance;
- **Payment**: it’s a cash-out which can be requested, approved or cancelled;
- **Bill**: a bill which is issued based on the Affiliate billing period performance. A bill can be further approved or cancelled;
- **Partner Debt Transaction**: if a partner has negative carryover feature enabled in the commission assigned to them, any debts are recorded as debt transactions.

You can filter the transactions by currency, transaction amount, date it was created, account type, reference type and its id as well as the actor of the transaction.

__Transaction examples with comments:__

**Example 1: **100 EUR was added to a Partner Account. Depending on the action reference, it may mean the following:

- *Partner balance increased due to Positive Balance Correction*
- *Partner balance increased because a bill was approved*
- *Partner balance increased because an Operator cancelled Affiliate’s cashout request.*

**Example 2**: - 100 EUR was removed from a Partner Account: 

- *Partner balance decreased due to Negative Balance Correction*
- *Partner balance decreased because an Affiliate requested a cashout or an Operator approved the cashout request*

**Example 3: **150 EUR was added to Bills Clip Account:

- *a Bill was issued to an Affiliate but wasn’t approved or cancelled yet*

**Example 4:** - 150 EUR was removed from Bills Clip Account:

- *a Bill was approved and paid to an Affiliate. As a result, it landed on Partner Account as a positive value.*
- *a Bill was cancelled. Funds were removed from Bills Clip Account, but Partner Account did not increase.*
